This site has gone through a few incarnations over the years. Each version is tagged in GitLab.

## 5.0.0 · Hugo custom theme · current (2026–)
![Version 5 — Hugo home page, dark](site-history/v5-dark.png)
![Version 5 — Hugo article, dark](site-history/v5-post.png)
A ground-up custom **Hugo** theme replacing PaperModX, with a Catppuccin/Fireside palette switcher, self-hosted Jost and Schibsted Grotesk, Neovim-framed code, picker-style search and build-time OG cards.

[Tag ↗](https://gitlab.com/hmajid2301/blog/-/tags/5.0.0)

## 4.0.0 · Hugo JamStack (2022–2026)
![Version 4 — Hugo home page, early](site-history/v4.png)
![Version 4 — Hugo blog post](site-history/v4-post.png)
![Version 4 — Hugo home page, latest](site-history/v4-latest.png)
Built with **Hugo** and the **PaperModX** theme, with content managed locally in the repo. I moved from Gatsby to Hugo because I no longer wanted to put in the effort to maintain the old site; the Gatsby build took about two minutes and Hugo builds in under a second. [Why I moved from Gatsby to Hugo](/posts/2022-11-17-why-i-moved-from-gatsby-to-hugo-for-this-blog/)

[Tag ↗](https://gitlab.com/hmajid2301/blog/-/tags/4.0.0)
