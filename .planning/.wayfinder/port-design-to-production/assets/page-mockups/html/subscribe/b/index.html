New to RSS?

Two minutes, once.

  1. Install a feed reader (most are free).
  2. Copy a feed URL from this page and add it as a subscription.
  3. New posts appear in the reader, in order, until you unsubscribe.

Readers people often use: NetNewsWire on Mac and iOS, Feedly or Inoreader in the browser, Miniflux if you self-host, and Newsboat in a terminal.
