Most readers can import an OPML file: one upload subscribes you to every feed above. Pick and choose afterwards.

<opml version="2.0">
  <head><title>haseebmajid.dev feeds</title></head>
  <body>
    <outline type="rss" text="Everything" xmlUrl="https://haseebmajid.dev/index.xml"/>
    <outline type="rss" text="Talks" xmlUrl="https://haseebmajid.dev/talks/index.xml"/>
