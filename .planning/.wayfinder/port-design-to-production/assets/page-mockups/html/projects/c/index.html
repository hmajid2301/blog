#Banter Bus

> Multiplayer party game

A browser-based multiplayer party game in the spirit of Jackbox or Skribbl.io, and my third attempt at building one.

State lives almost entirely on the server: HTML is rendered with templ, HTMX swaps it in, and Alpine covers the small bits of client-side interactivity. Deployed to my homelab k3s cluster with FluxCD.

Go, templ, HTMX, Alpine.js, Tailwind, Postgres, WebSockets
