## Design review (`hugo server` + `playwright-cli`, 1440 and 390 wide)

### Also leftover from design-lab

- **Hardcoded post art.** `layouts/_default/single.html:36-40` maps three URLs to `editorial-art.html` SVGs. The RLS post therefore shows a large illustration captioned "Original SVG cover exploration." with a "Download SVG ↗" link. Either turn it into a real front-matter feature (`art: row-level-security`, with no "exploration" caption or download link) or remove it.
- **Two progress bars.** The reading progress bar is blue (not the accent colour) and draws on top of a static full-width mauve strip. Keep one bar, in `--accent`.

### Typography

- **Body text looks faint and airy.** The colour isn't the problem: it measures 7:1 or better in every palette (see Colours). The cause is Jost's thin strokes at 19px/400, plus line-height close to 2 and lines of about 85 characters. Suggestions:
  - Jost weight 450–500 (it's a variable font), or a sturdier body face;
  - line-height about 1.7;
  - line length capped at about 68ch.
- **Post titles are too big.** At 64–72px with tight tracking, a normal title takes 3 lines and the Lanzaboote title takes 5, pushing the article below the fold. Use `clamp(2.25rem, 4vw, 3.5rem)` for post `h1`, and keep the huge size for the home hero only.
- **Headings switch fonts.** Body `h2`s are Schibsted Grotesk ("Introduction"), but `/uses` renders "What do I use?" in Jost medium. Apply the heading font to every `.post-content h1–h4`.
- **The mono font is barely visible.** Eyebrows and dates at 10–11px mono are hard to read in light mode (contrast is close to AA failure). Use 12px minimum and the `subtext0` colour, or bolder.

### Post header

- **Rebuild the meta block** as one line: `31 Dec 2025 · 12 min read · 2,488 words`, followed by a separate row of tag chips.
  - Drop the commas, the stray `·` and the "0 Views".
  - Put the edit icon at the bottom of the post ("Suggest an edit"), not in the header.
  - Tag chips should be lowercase `#nixos`, matching how tags are named, instead of `#Nixos`.
- **Breadcrumbs** `Home » Posts` look like PaperMod leftovers. Either drop them (the header nav already highlights "Posts") or restyle them as the mono eyebrow used elsewhere (`POSTS / NIXOS`).
- **The table of contents** is a full-width pill that's collapsed by default, so it reads as an odd button. On wide screens (≥1200px), make it a sticky right-hand column that highlights the current section. On narrow screens, use a plain disclosure without the pill border.

### Lists and cards

- **Two list styles.** `/posts/` and tag pages use big filled PaperMod cards, with a full summary, tags and word count on every card. The home page uses the cleaner date-column rows, and archive uses ruled lines. Pick one: the home "date column + title + one-line summary" row would work for posts, tags and related posts. It's also the cheapest to maintain.
- **Underlined titles.** Talks timeline titles are underlined in addition to the hover arrow. Drop the underline, or show it on hover only, as the home page lists already do.
- **The tags page is a wall of about 300 chips**, many with 1 post and several near-duplicates (`flake`/`flakes`, `go`/`golang`, `home lab`/`homelab`, `ci`/`ci-cd`/`CD`). Suggestions:
  - merge the duplicate tags in content;
  - show the "top topics" (≥5 posts) larger first and the long tail as a compact list;
  - left-align the tag grid with the title (it's currently indented about 50px).

### Home

- **The hero card has dead space** on the right under the photo, and the social icons have no labels or tooltips. Options:
  - tighten the card's padding;
  - align the photo's top with the eyebrow;
  - add `aria-label` and `title` to the icons;
  - consider a single "Latest" pull-quote instead of repeating 6 full post cards that the posts list already shows.
- **Duplicate talks.** Talks I've given lists "What I learnt building a web app with Go and HTMX" twice (GopherCon UK and GoLab). Group talks given more than once: one title, with conference chips.

### Mobile (390px)

- The header wraps to two rows (logo, then nav plus actions), using about 120px. Try one row with the logo mark only plus nav, or a compact menu.
- The hero eyebrow wraps to two lines. Shorten it on small screens (`HASEEB MAJID` only).
- The post meta collapses into a vertical stack with orphaned `·` (covered by the meta rebuild above).

### Polish

- **Theme toggle:** replace the ☀️/🌙 emoji with the same line-icon set as the search and social icons.
- **Code block surface:** see Colours #3.
- **404 page:** give it the same treatment as `/uses`, a terminal fence: `❯ cd /this-page` / `cd: no such file or directory`, then links to Posts, Talks and search.
- **Dev-only console error:** opening the site via `127.0.0.1` makes search fetch `localhost:1313/index.json` and fail CORS. `fastsearch.js` should fetch a relative `/index.json`.

## Colours

Measured with `playwright-cli run-code` on a post, in all four palette/mode combinations. Each entry is foreground on background with its WCAG contrast ratio.

| Element | Catppuccin light | Catppuccin dark | Fireside light | Fireside dark |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Body / headings | `#4c4f69`/`#eff1f5` **7.06** | `#cdd6f4`/`#1e1e2e` **11.34** | `#3c2b26`/`#faf2e8` **12.09** | `#f4e8da`/`#281f1a` **13.37** |
| Links / nav (accent) | `#8031e0` **5.41** | `#cba6f7` **8.07** | `#a13e25` **5.86** | `#f0aa7d` **8.26** |
| Tag chips (muted, 12px) | 5.53 | 9.26 | 5.89 | 8.26 |
| Neovim status bar (11px) | 5.14 | 9.91 | 5.19 | 7.06 |
| **Code comments** | `#9ca0b0` **2.14 ✗** | `#6c7086` **3.59 ✗** | `#928374` **2.91 ✗** | `#928374` **3.76 ✗** |

**What works.** Body text, headings, links and muted text all pass AA in all four palettes. The Catppuccin Latte accent is already darkened (`#8031e0` instead of the official `#8839ef`), which is the right call. Fireside has the best reading contrast of the four.

**Problems:**

1. **Code comments fail contrast everywhere.** The worst is Catppuccin light at 2.14:1. Comments are often the most important line in a blog snippet. Use `subtext0` for comments (Latte `#6c6f85` ≈ 4.6:1, Mocha `#a6adc8` ≈ 7:1), and a similar lift for Fireside's `#928374`, while keeping the italics.
2. **Fireside dark code is partly unreadable.** Plain identifiers (`plants p`, `p.name;`) render in a dark slate blue on brown. It looks like Catppuccin Latte's text colour leaking from `assets/css/lib/catppuccin-latte.css` / `highlight.scss` into the Fireside dark block. Fireside also uses Gruvbox syntax colours (`#3c3836`, `#ebdbb2`) on top of its own brown palette. That's fine as long as it's deliberate and complete: map every Chroma token for `fireside-dark`.
3. **Code blocks don't stand out from the page.** The editor body uses the page background in all four palettes (`#eff1f5`/`#1e1e2e`/`#faf2e8`/`#281f1a`), so only a 1px border separates code from prose. Give the editor body its own surface: Catppuccin `mantle` in light, `crust` in dark, and the Fireside `surface` equivalents.
4. **Cards go in opposite directions.** In Catppuccin dark, cards are *darker* than the page (`surface #181825` on `bg #1e1e2e`). In Fireside dark they're *lighter* (`#382a22` on `#281f1a`). Pick one rule; lighter, i.e. raised, is the more common convention for dark UIs.
5. **The colour system is hard to read and carries dead weight.** `zzz-playground-palettes.css` has 49 palette rules (journal, workshop, studio, notebook, swiss, nord, gruvbox, terminal, ink, frappé, macchiato, plus `data-accent` overrides). Only `cat-*` and `fireside-*` are used. The default `html[data-theme]` block even sets a rose `#a43b54` accent and `#f38ba8` dark accent that are always overridden. Replace it with four explicit blocks (`[data-theme=cat|fireside][data-mode=light|dark]`), each defining the same token names. This matches the ticket 03 decision.
6. **The accent appears in too many places.** It's used for links, nav, eyebrows, the arrow icons, the Neovim mode, the progress bar, the photo frame and the footer switch, so nothing stands out. Keep it for links, the current nav item and one highlight per screen. Use `muted` for eyebrows and arrows.
7. **The progress bar.** At the top of a post the bar already shows full width, and a second blue bar grows over it as you scroll (seen at 1440px). Use one bar, in `--lab-accent`, and make it 2–3px instead of 4.

### Recommended Neovim block (desktop)

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ NORMAL  1  query.sql                                       ▍ lettucego │  tabline (mantle)
│ 󰉋 db › queries › query.sql › ListPlants                                │  winbar (base, muted)
│   3 │ -- name: ListPlants :many                                        │
│   2 │ SELECT *                                                         │  relative numbers,
│   1 │ FROM plants p                                                    │  current line absolute + bright
│ W 4 │ WHERE p.user_id = $1     ● filter moved into RLS policy          │  sign + virtual-text annotation
│   1 │ ORDER BY p.name;                                                 │
│  NORMAL │  query.sql │  main            │ sqls │ 4:1 │ 100% │  statusline (crust)
│ "query.sql" 5L, 98B                                                    │  cmdline → becomes "5 lines yanked" on copy
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Tabline:** green `NORMAL` pill, then a peach `1` badge with a file-type icon and the filename, then a project badge on the right. The filename comes from a fence attribute (```` ```sql {file="db/queries/query.sql"} ````); fall back to `main.<ext>` from the language. Use the project badge for the post's repo, e.g. `lettucego` (an optional `repo` field in front matter), or leave it out.
- **Winbar:** the breadcrumb path is built from `file`, shown in muted text with `›` separators. Hide it when there's no `file`.
- **Gutter:** relative numbers, with the "cursor" on the first `hl_lines` line (absolute number, bright text, cursorline background). With no `hl_lines`, show no cursorline and absolute numbers in `overlay0`. That fixes the "line 1 always highlighted" bug.
- **Statusline:** blue `NORMAL` pill, file icon + name, dim branch, then on the right a blue LSP pill **chosen by language** (`go→gopls`, `nix→nil_ls`, `lua→lua_ls`, `ts→vtsls`, `py→basedpyright`, `sql→sqls`, `bash→bashls`), `L:C`, and `%`. The language-to-LSP map is a small `data/` file. Drop the "N lines" duplicate.
- **Cmdline row:** `"query.sql" 5L, 98B`. When the copy button is pressed, this row changes to **`5 lines yanked`**, which is the Neovim message. Put the copy button in the tabline (`⎘`).
- **Annotations (new):** an optional fence attribute (```` {notes="4:filter moved into RLS policy"} ````) renders as italic diagnostic-style text after that line, with a `W`/`I`/`H` sign in the gutter (warning, info, hint). Optional, but it's the most "this is my actual editor" detail.
- **TODO/INFO badges:** comments starting with `TODO:` / `INFO:` / `NOTE:` / `FIX:` get the todo-comments badge (green/mauve/blue/red). This is a CSS or render-hook regex on Chroma comment spans.
- **Indent guides and colorcolumn:** skip them; they're costly to render server-side and add little.
- **Mobile:** keep only the tabline and the code. Hide the winbar and cmdline, and shrink the statusline to `NORMAL │ 4:1`.
- **Colours:** use the official Mocha values for every Neovim surface in dark mode (`mantle` tabline, `base` editor, `crust` statusline), so the blog matches the real screenshots. In light mode use Latte with the same mapping.

### Terminal: match the floating terminal, not a Ghostty window

Ticket 02 chose a Ghostty-style window, but the floating terminal from the screenshot (`editor.png`) is more distinctive and consistent with the Neovim block:

- a rounded 1px border in the **accent** colour, with the title set into the top border (`╭─ ~/dotfiles ──────╮`), set via a fence attribute (` ```terminal {title="~/projects/blog"} `), defaulting to `~`;

- the Nerd Font icons need a self-hosted subset of **Symbols Nerd Font Mono** (nixicle's kitty config already maps it, `modules/aspects/cli/terminals/kitty.nix:7`), or the `nitch` output on `/uses` keeps showing `⊠` boxes.

### Accent colour: lavender or mauve?

The system accent is **lavender** (`#b4befe` Mocha), but the blog uses mauve. Lavender would tie the blog to the screenshots. In light mode, Latte lavender `#7287fd` is only 2.81:1 on `#eff1f5`, so it needs darkening for link text (`#4a5ad8` measures 4.96:1, which passes AA; Mocha lavender `#b4befe` on `#1e1e2e` is 9.17:1), the same way mauve was darkened to `#8031e0`. ## Performance, feeds and other non-design issues

Measured on the full `hugo` build (`public/`-equivalent in `/tmp/ogbuild`).

**Images (the biggest win).** The site ships **987 images, 205 MB**.

- **GIFs are huge.** The five largest are 2.9–11.4 MB, e.g. `2022-12-02-my-useful-vim-commands-with-vs-code/images/nerd_tree.gif` (11.4 MB) and `indent_outdent.gif` (5.8 MB). Convert them to `.mp4`/`.webm` (`ffmpeg -i x.gif -movflags faststart -pix_fmt yuv420p x.mp4`), which is usually 90%+ smaller. `render-image.html` already renders video resources as `<video>`, so the markdown only needs the new extension. Use `autoplay muted loop playsinline` for GIF-like behaviour.
- **No resizing, no WebP, no `width`/`height`.** `render-image.html` outputs the original file with `loading="lazy"` but no dimensions. Images therefore load at full size and the page jumps as they arrive. Use Hugo's `.Process "webp"` with a `srcset` (e.g. 720/1440w) and output `width`/`height` from the resource. Only 7 WebP files exist today.
- **Unused cover images.** 228 legacy tcardgen `cover.png` files (**77 MB**) are still deployed, and nothing shows them once the OG card replaces them. Deleting them is a ticket 08 item (already noted there).
- Alt text is good: all 249 post images have meaningful alt text.

**RSS.**

- **Relative links break in feed readers.** Feeds include full post HTML, but internal links stay relative (`href="./posts/2024-09-06-…"`), 22 of them in `index.xml`. Readers resolve those against the feed URL or not at all. Make links absolute in the RSS template (`replaceRE` on `href="./` → `{{ site.BaseURL }}`), or have the markdown link render hook output `.Permalink` for internal links.
- The feed's channel description is the generic "Recent content on Haseeb Majid". Set it from `site.Params.description` / the tagline.

**Analytics and privacy.**

- **Two analytics scripts run on every page**: GoatCounter (`gc.zgo.at/count.js`) *and* self-hosted Plausible. Pick one. GoatCounter powers the "N Views" counter (which shows "0 Views" until it loads, and locally always). If the counter stays, keep GoatCounter and drop Plausible, or vice versa and drop the counter.
- **Google Fonts** is loaded from `fonts.googleapis.com`, which sends each visitor's IP to Google, adds a third-party connection and causes a font swap. Self-host Jost and Schibsted (woff2, subset to Latin) with `font-display: swap` and size-matched fallbacks.
- **YouTube** embeds on talk pages load `youtube.com` straight away. The "lite YouTube" component (see Components #5) fixes privacy and weight together.

**SEO.**

- The design-lab pages are in `sitemap.xml` (4 entries). They're removed with the strip step; until then add `build.list = "never"` / `sitemap.disable = true` to their front matter.
- JSON-LD `image` points to the legacy cover (covered in ticket 09, Phase 4).
- Titles in `<title>` and `og:title` end in `| Haseeb Majid`, which is fine. Posts don't set `description`, so meta descriptions fall back to `.Summary`, which starts with heading text ("Introduction In this article…"). Use Hugo's `summaryLength` together with `<!--more-->`, or add `description:` to the most-shared posts.

**Content hygiene** (not design, but it shows on every list):

- Merge duplicate tags (`go`/`golang`, `flake`/`flakes`, `home lab`/`homelab`, `ci`/`ci-cd`/`CD`), and lowercase tags consistently. The tag chips show `#Nixos` because the tag display is title-cased.
- Two talks have the same title (Go + HTMX at GopherCon UK and GoLab; Nix dev envs at London Gophers and GoLab). Group them, or add the venue to the title.

## Missing features worth considering

- **Fonts.** Jost and Schibsted load from Google Fonts with no fallback size matching, so the layout can shift as they load. MonoLisa is named in the font stack, but visitors don't have it. Self-host the fonts and use JetBrains Mono as the fallback.
- **Code blocks.** A filename/title label (```` ```go {title="main.go"} ```` shown in the Neovim tab), plus line-number links.
- **Talks.** The same talk given at two conferences shows as near-duplicate entries; consider grouping them.
- **Series.** Previous/next navigation within a series and a "Part 3 of 5" label in the post header (133 posts use `series`).
- **Callouts.** A styled callout/admonition shortcode; posts already hand-roll "Be careful" boxes.
- **Accessibility.** A "skip to content" link, and a contrast check of the small mono text, especially in Fireside light.
- **Changelog.** The 5.0.0 entry on the site-history page (required by ticket 07).
